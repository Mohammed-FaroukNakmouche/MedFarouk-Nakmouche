Mohammed Farouk Nakmouche

RF / Microwave / Millimeter-Wave Researcher · Antennas & Waveguides · AI-Assisted Electromagnetic Design · Additive Manufacturing

I am a researcher in electrical engineering working on antennas, passive microwave and millimeter-wave components, and compact high-frequency hardware. My research combines electromagnetic design with machine learning to accelerate the synthesis and optimization of antennas, substrate-integrated waveguides, printed ridge gap waveguides, and fully metallic ridge gap waveguides.

My work also explores additive and advanced manufacturing for low-cost, compact, and non-conventional RF front ends, including 3-D-printed waveguide components, reconfigurable antennas, MIMO systems, and antenna-in-package concepts for emerging wireless and Internet-of-Space applications.

I have contributed to research projects in Canada, Algeria, France, Turkey, Egypt, Taiwan, and Singapore, spanning academic research, industrial R&D, and early-stage technology development.
